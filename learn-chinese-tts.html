Pick Your Lane First

Trying to learn speaking and reading at the same time splits your attention and slows both. Pick one track first, build confidence, then layer the other on top.

Speaking / Understanding

Focus on pinyin + tones. Skip characters at first. Use TTS, listen and repeat. Train your ear before your eyes.

Tip:
Reveal on hover for self-testing
Reading / Writing

Focus on characters. Each one is its own memorization project (~2,000-3,000 to read a newspaper). Attach sounds to shapes you recognize.

Tip:
Use the Deck view to self-test
