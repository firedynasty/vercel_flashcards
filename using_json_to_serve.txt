⏺ That's all three pieces done:

  1. generate_sheets.py — run python generate_sheets.py workbook.xlsx → produces
   sheets.json, then upload to Dropbox with rclone
  2. api/sheets.js — proxies DROPBOX_SHEETS_URL, same pattern as the video
  viewer
  3. learn-chinese-tts.html — on page load, fetches /api/sheets, populates a
  dropdown in the sticky bar, auto-selects the first sheet matching "chinese",
  and loads it. No sign-in needed.

  The Google Sheets sign-in still works as a fallback if you want to load
  something not in the xlsx. The dropdown sits at the left of the sticky bar so
  you can switch sheets anytime.
